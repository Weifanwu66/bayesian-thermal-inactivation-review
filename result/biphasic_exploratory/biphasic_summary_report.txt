INTERPRETATION AND RECOMMENDATION
==================================================================

Across all 169 model-eligible curves (113 infectivity, 56 PCR),
10 (5.9%) were classified as potentially biphasic using the
heuristic criteria (early/late slope ratio >= 3x, >= 2 late-phase
slope segments, genuine inactivation in both phases).

Classification distinguishes between:
  * Approximately log-linear: relatively constant reduction rate
  * Smooth shoulder/tailing : monotonically changing slope (Weibull n!=1)
  * Potentially biphasic    : distinct early rapid + late slow kinetic phases
  * Insufficient information: too few observed non-censored time points

IMPORTANT: A curve is NOT classified as biphasic merely because its
last point tails or because one point deviates from the trajectory.

Recommendation:

  visual inspection confirms clearly biphasic trajectories in
